# Creative Engineer identity

The mark combines an open **C** frame, three structured **E** rails, and a rust-colored point representing human judgment inside a technical system.

## Files

- `creative-engineer-symbol.svg`: primary transparent symbol.
- `creative-engineer-symbol-mono.svg`: one-color use, including stamps and engraving.
- `creative-engineer-symbol-mono-white.svg`: one-color use on dark backgrounds.
- `creative-engineer-symbol-reversed.svg`: symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `creative-engineer-logo-horizontal.svg`: primary wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `creative-engineer-logo-horizontal-reversed.svg`: wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `creative-engineer-app-icon.svg`: favicon and interface avatar.
- `creative-engineer-profile-512.png`: square profile image.
- `creative-engineer-email-logo.png`: email-safe logo on white.
- `creative-engineer-email-signature.png`: email-safe signature card on white.

## Usage

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of its decision point. Use the symbol no smaller than 24 px and the horizontal logo no smaller than 180 px wide. Do not redraw, rotate, stretch, recolor, add effects, or place it on a low-contrast background.

For Gmail or another email client, upload `creative-engineer-email-signature.png` as the signature image and display it at approximately 300–420 px wide. The white background is intentional: it keeps the colors and text predictable across light and dark email themes. Use `creative-engineer-email-logo.png` when only the logo is needed.

## Palette

- Pale Mist: `#DCE6E7`
